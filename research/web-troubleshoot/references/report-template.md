# Investigation report template

Use selectively, in the user's language. Lead with decisions and evidence; do not fill every section mechanically or turn placeholders into invented facts. Link citations next to the claims they support. For a short investigation, merge sections while preserving uncertainties and next checks.

## Current conclusion and immediate next step

State the best-supported explanation or unresolved alternatives, its confidence and basis, and the next useful action. Say whether this is an online analogy, locally supported hypothesis, reproduced mechanism, or verified correction.

## Problem fingerprint

Summarize expected/observed behavior, impact, timeline and timezone, relevant environment/versions, recent changes, and prior attempts. Label user reports, inspected facts, and unknowns.

## External cases that matter

| Case and direct source | Environment and date/version | Observed mechanism and outcome | Applicability and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|

Include consequential failed attempts and contradictory findings. A source's observations and its explanations may have different confidence. Avoid padding this table with repeated copies of one report.

## Differences and missing prerequisites

| Dimension | Online case / authoritative requirement | Our evidence | Match / difference / unknown | Why it matters and next check |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Distinguish genuine requirements from optional recommendations and candidate explanations. Identify gaps in our evidence separately from confirmed gaps in our implementation or operations.

## Ranked hypotheses and discriminating checks

| Hypothesis and confidence | Supporting / contradicting evidence | Cheapest next check | Expected result / implication of the opposite result |
|---|---|---|---|

If local verification is unavailable, state exactly what observation is needed. Do not call a hypothesis confirmed solely because several online cases resemble it.

## Ordered action path

For the few actions that matter, specify:

- Purpose and dependencies; containment, workaround, correction, or prevention.
- Concrete check or proposed change, applicable environment/version, and evidence supporting it.
- Expected outcome, verification signal, and what to do if the result contradicts the hypothesis.
- Material side effects, operational cost, and abort/recovery conditions where relevant.
- Status: recommended, performed, or verified; any authorization or access still required.

If unresolved, provide a minimum reproduction or sanitized support-case outline when useful. Do not send it externally without authorization.

## Coverage, limits, and continuation

Briefly state the causal branches and source types researched, the primary evidence traced, inaccessible material, and remaining contradictions or unknowns. Record the next targeted search/check if the investigation must continue. Do not claim comprehensive coverage of the internet.
